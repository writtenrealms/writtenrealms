Ternium Rules
The rules for death in Ternium are different than for the rest of Edeus. If you die here, all of your equipped items will be PERMANENTLY DESTROYED. This cannot be reversed or undone regardless of circumstances! Proceed at your own risk.

* If you understand, enter 'I understand, let me in.'
* If that is too spicy for you, type 'leave'

This is a difficult dungeon, and you should do it as a group. To enter the dungeon as a group, make sure all members are grouped and in the same room when the leader uses the 'enter' command , and everyone will be taken to the same instance.

Once already in an instance, the leader can use the 'invite' command to bring additional players in.
