# Founder’s Passes

A Founder’s Pass gives one account free access to a particular world for as long
as that world operates. It does not cover other worlds or remove normal conduct
rules and usage limits. This optional program is available only on participating
installations and worlds.

## Receive and claim a pass

You receive an email when an administrator grants you a pass. Its address is
admitted to an invite-only installation immediately. Sign in or sign up with
that address, then open **Account → Founder’s Passes**. An invitation may reserve
your account before your first visit; use the
normal email login link to verify it. You can set your profile name and newsletter
preference in Account after signing in.

A direct pass starts unclaimed. Choose **Claim on this account** to keep it and
unlock your one +1 invitation. This permanently prevents transferring the pass
to another account. Merely signing in or playing does not claim it.

Unclaimed passes automatically become claimed when the operator’s beta cutoff
arrives, even if their recipients have never signed in. The pass page displays
the cutoff in your local timezone, or says that no deadline has been set yet.

## Transfer an unclaimed direct pass

Before claiming, expand **Transfer to another account** to send a transfer
confirmation to another email. The
recipient must confirm that link within 24 hours and before the beta cutoff.
Until then, the original account keeps the pass and can cancel the transfer or
claim it. Sending a new transfer confirmation invalidates the previous link.

Confirmation moves the pass **and its unused +1 invitation** to the destination
account and claims it immediately. It cannot be transferred again. Characters,
inventory, and progress remain on the original account. The original account
may lose invite-only site access if the pass was its only invitation.

If you only want to replace the email on your existing account, use **Account →
Change email address** instead. Verify the new address using the emailed link;
your account, characters, and passes stay together, including already claimed
or gifted passes. The new address must not belong to another account. Previous
linked sign-ins are disconnected; use the new email to sign in afterward.

## Invite your +1

After claiming a direct pass, choose **Invite your +1** and confirm the destination
email carefully. This grants a gifted Founder’s Pass, already claimed on the
recipient’s account, and admits that email to the site. The recipient cannot
transfer it or invite someone else. Your unused invitation remains available
after beta.

An address that already received a pass cannot consume another invitation for
the same world. A retry cannot duplicate a pass or reset an invitation. Ask the
administrator for help with a mistaken address or a missing notification.
