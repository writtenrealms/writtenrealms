# Subscriptions & passes

Open **Account → Subscriptions & passes** to see world access attached to your
account. This section is available to every signed-in player.

Paid subscriptions are not available yet. If you have no subscriptions or passes,
you can return to the lobby to play, or browse worlds on a multi-world installation.
There is nothing to purchase or set up on this page right now.

## Your passes

Any passes you receive appear as tickets, with their world and pass type together
in the title and artwork from the world's lobby when available. Each ticket
explains its access and available actions. A configured
claim deadline appears when applicable. Only passes your account holds
are shown; the page is not a catalog of pass programs.

Follow the instructions on your pass before claiming, transferring, or sending
an invitation. Review any destination email carefully before confirming an action.
Once claimed, the ticket shows **Claimed YYYY/MM/DD** beneath the divider, using
your local date. This records ownership, not necessarily when you could first play.

Existing passes remain visible if the operator pauses new issuance. A pass
covers the world named on it; normal conduct rules and usage limits still apply.

## Changing your email

Use **Account → Change email address** to update the address on the same account.
Verify the new address using the emailed link. Your characters, progress, and
passes stay with you. Changing your account email is separate from transferring
a pass to another account.
